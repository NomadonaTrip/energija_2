About

Powering Nigeria's businesses with intelligent energy.

Cleantech Supply Chain · est. 2018

Energija is a cleantech supply chain operator. We source intelligent lighting hardware from premium European manufacturers, then design, install, and maintain it across Nigerian commercial and industrial sites — turning energy waste into a measurable operating advantage.

Most commercial spaces still rely on manual lighting control — switching off by hand, inconsistent schedules, or guesswork energy rationing. The result is wasted power, unpredictable bills, and declining profitability.

Our technical expertise and deep knowledge of Nigeria's energy infrastructure set us apart from one-size-fits-all alternatives. We offer flexible pricing, a wide product range from motion-sensor controls to complete LED retrofits, and responsive nationwide support.
